# PuzzleProof: proving chess puzzles in Lean 4

**[Step through the proof of the GothamChess mate in 11](https://elanroth.github.io/PuzzleProof/viewer/gotham.html)**

The goal is to prove statements such as

```lean
OnlyWinningMove Puzzles.gotham (uci "b7b2") ∧ WinsIn .black 11 Puzzles.gotham
```

with the Lean kernel as the checker. Both are now proved, kernel only, in `ChessDemo/Gotham.lean`
(the Stage 1 closing demonstration). So is the exact distance, `MateIn .black 11 Puzzles.gotham`
(mate in 11 and not in 10), in `ChessDemoBound/GothamMateIn.lean`: its lower half is a generated
certificate that refutes every Black move at every Black turn
([`docs/research/minimality.md`](docs/research/minimality.md)). All of these come with their reading through the rules alone
(`SpecWinsIn`, `SpecOnlyWinningMove`, `SpecMateIn`). The trusted file under that reading,
`Chess/Spec/Winning.lean`, and the bridge `Chess/ChessGame/Fide.lean` have had an independent review
(`docs/reviews/G8_REVIEW.md`: no soundness defect). Stockfish is only an untrusted source of
certificates.
The rules of chess are written as a readable specification. Every definition has an English
docstring, and that docstring also appears in `docs/spec/`. Executable code (the move generator,
the search, the certificate checkers) is linked to the specification by proved theorems.

Lean 4.28.0, core and Std only (no Mathlib). Status (2026-11-03, after a full rebuild of every
target: 216 jobs, 38 min on 10 cores): `lake build`, `lake build ChessTest`, `lake build ChessDemo`,
`lake build ChessDemoBound` and `lake build chess-perft` are green, with no warnings. There is no
`sorry`, no `axiom` and no `native_decide` anywhere, and every headline theorem uses only `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` (checked by `#guard_msgs` in `ChessDemo.Axioms`,
`ChessDemoBound.GothamMateIn` and `ChessTest.Axioms`). The generator is proved to list exactly the legal moves (M4) and none twice (M5). See
[`docs/VERIFICATION.md`](docs/VERIFICATION.md) for what was proved, tested and audited, and
[`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) for where things stand and what comes next.

## Build

```sh
lake build                 # the library: about 8 s from clean
lake build ChessTest       # tests, perft suite, axioms ledger: about 3 min, peaks near 4 GB
lake build ChessTest.Axioms  # the axioms ledger of the library's theorems alone: seconds
lake build ChessTest.Interface  # the signatures generated certificate files rely on: seconds
lake build ChessDemo       # the Gotham puzzle and small mates: about 3 min, peaks near 3 GB
lake build ChessDemoBound  # mate in exactly 11: about 45 min on 10 cores, under 1 GB per process
lake build chess-perft     # the perft program, for differential tests:
.lake/build/bin/chess-perft "rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 1" 4
```

To step through the Gotham proof on a board, open the viewer:
**https://elanroth.github.io/PuzzleProof/viewer/gotham.html** (the same page as `viewer/gotham.html`,
built by `scripts/build_viewer.py`; see `scripts/README.md`).

The Gotham certificate is regenerated by `scripts/gen_cert.py` (Stockfish and python-chess; see
`scripts/README.md`).

In VS Code, install the **lean4** extension (`leanprover.lean4`) and open the repository
root, where `lakefile.toml` is. The prototype in `prototype/` is a separate Lake project; open that
folder on its own to work on it. Run
`lake build` once before opening files, so that imports load from `.lake/` instead of being
rebuilt in the editor. Put the cursor on a theorem to see its goal in the Infoview. The tests of
`Rules`, `MoveGen`, `ChessGame`, `ChessGame/Fide` and `Puzzles` are in `ChessTest/`, with the perft
suite and the axioms ledgers; those modules keep only a few cheap `#guard`s as documentation.
`Basic` and `Position` keep their tests in a `## Tests` section at their bottom, and `Game` in
`Chess/Game/Examples.lean`.

## What to read first

Read in this order to audit a chess headline. Items 2 to 6, with the Lean kernel, are the trusted
base: everything a reader must check to believe a headline. Everything else is linked to it by
proved theorems. Two caveats remain (ROADMAP §2): FIDE's draw rules are absent (FEN clocks are
ignored, THEOREMS G15), and `Position.Sane` admits some unreachable positions.

1. **`docs/ROADMAP.md`**: current state, how faithful the English↔Lean match is, risks, and the
   next steps. Its §2 ends with the trusted base of a chess headline, which this list follows.
2. **`Chess/Basic.lean`**: colours, kinds, pieces, squares and the geometry of rays.
3. **`Chess/Position.lean`**: boards, moves (in UCI shape), castling rights and positions.
4. **`Chess/Rules/Defs.lean`**, next to **`docs/spec/Rules.md`**: the definitions of the rules,
   meaning what "attacks", "check", "legal move", "checkmate" and "playing a move" mean, and
   nothing else. Every docstring there is meant to be checked against the FIDE Laws by a chess
   player. The lemmas (`Chess/Rules/Laws.lean`, `Castling.lean`) are proofs, not trusted.
5. **`Chess/Spec/Winning.lean`** (five definitions, about 45 lines with docstrings): `SpecForces`,
   and what `SpecWinsIn`, `SpecWins`, `SpecMateIn` and `SpecOnlyWinningMove` mean, written with the
   rules alone.
6. **The notation in `Chess/ChessGame.lean`**: the writers (section "Writing"), the predicates
   `IsNumeral`, `ClockFields`, `IsFEN` and `Position.Sane` (section "Accepting"), and the
   statements of `fen_eq_iff` and `uci_eq_iff`. These say what `fen "…"` and `uci "…"` denote.
   Then the FEN string itself: `Puzzles.gotham` is one line of `Chess/Puzzles.lean`.

A headline is stated with `Chess.WinsIn`, `Chess.OnlyWinningMove` and the other abbreviations of
`Chess/ChessGame.lean`. It is read as `SpecWinsIn`, `SpecOnlyWinningMove`, … through the proved
equivalences of `Chess/ChessGame/Fide.lean` (THEOREMS G8), so the reader need not trust how those
abbreviations are defined.

**How claims are computed, not what they mean.** These are not in the trusted base of a chess
headline; each is linked to it by proved theorems:

- `Chess/MoveGen.lean`: the move generator, proved sound and complete (M4: `mem_legalMoves`).
- `Chess/Game/Defs.lean` (130 lines, over half of them docstrings): `WinsIn`, `MateIn` and
  `OnlyWinningMove` for any two-player game. It is the meaning of these words for an abstract game,
  but a chess headline no longer depends on it. The other files of `Chess/Game/` are proofs
  (`Laws`), the bounded search (`Search`), the certificate checkers with their diagnostics
  (`Cert`), and toy examples (`Examples`).
- `Game.chess` and the four abbreviations in `Chess/ChessGame.lean`: chess as a game of
  `Chess.Game`. The tests in `ChessTest/ChessGame.lean` show headline-style statements (a mate in
  1, Fool's mate, a stalemate).
- `Chess/ChessGame/Fide.lean`: the equivalences named above.

Then `docs/DESIGN.md` (architecture and the reasons for it) and `docs/THEOREMS.md` (the target
theorems, tiers R/M/G/C/O, stages 1–5). THEOREMS predates some renames. The name map at the end of
the roadmap covers them.

## File map

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Chess.lean` | Root; imports every module. Its docstring repeats the trusted/untrusted map above. |
| `Chess/Basic.lean` | Colours, kinds, pieces, squares (`Fin 8 × Fin 8`), offsets, directions, rays, castling squares. |
| `Chess/Position.lean` | `Board` (a function from squares), packed board literals, `Move` (UCI shape), `CastlingRights`, `Position`. |
| `Chess/Rules.lean`, `Chess/Rules/` | **Trusted FIDE spec**, in three files under one umbrella: `Defs` (trusted, definitions only: `Reaches`, `AttackedBy`, `InCheck`, the six move rules, `PseudoLegal`, `Position.play`, `Legal`, `Checkmate`, `Stalemate`), `Laws` (R3, R4, R5, R8, R11 and the bridges), `Castling` (L4.11). |
| `Chess/MoveGen.lean` | `legalMoves` (geometry proposes, the rules dispose; proved sound and complete, M4), perft, and agreement tests against the spec. |
| `Chess/Game.lean`, `Chess/Game/` | Abstract games, in five files under one umbrella: `Defs` (`Forces`, `WinsIn`, `MateIn`, `OnlyWinningMove`; the meaning for an abstract game, not trusted for chess headlines since G8), `Laws`, `Search` (proved correct), `Cert` (`Cert` and `OnlyMoveCert` with checkers proved sound, the win checker also complete, glue for per-node proofs, and diagnostics that locate a rejection; the interface of generated certificate files), `Examples`. |
| `Chess/ChessGame.lean` | `Game.chess`, the `Chess.WinsIn`/… abbreviations, and FEN/UCI read and write with proved round trips. The notation (writers, `IsFEN`, `Position.Sane`, the statements of `fen_eq_iff`, `uci_eq_iff`) is trusted; the rest is not. |
| `Chess/Spec/Winning.lean` | **Trusted** winning written with the rules alone (`SpecWinsIn`, …), for reading chess headlines without the generator or `Chess.Game`. |
| `Chess/ChessGame/Fide.lean` | G8: the chess-game notions are equivalent to those of `Spec/Winning`. |
| `Chess/Puzzles.lean` | **Trusted**: the positions of headline theorems, named once, one FEN string each (`Puzzles.gotham`). |
| `ChessTest/` | The test library `ChessTest` (not built by `lake build`): the tests of `Rules`, `MoveGen`, `ChessGame`, `ChessGame/Fide`; `Perft.lean` and `PerftDeep.lean` (the six standard perft positions); `Axioms.lean` and `AxiomsPerft.lean` (`#print axioms` on every headline theorem, checked; the perft theorems in the second); `Interface.lean` (the signatures generated certificate files use, checked); `Puzzles.lean` (the tests of `Chess/Puzzles.lean`); `Tabulate.lean` (`Position.tabulate`, a board read once into an array, with `tabulate_eq`). |
| `ChessDemo/` | The demo library `ChessDemo` (not built by `lake build`): `GothamCert.lean` (generated by `scripts/gen_cert.py`: 319 per-node theorems), `Gotham.lean` (the headline theorems), `Mates.lean` (exact mates in 1 and 2 by search; in 3 by a certificate for the win and a search for minimality), `Axioms.lean` (their `#print axioms`, checked). |
| `ChessDemoBound/` | The library `ChessDemoBound` (not built by `lake build`; `lake build ChessDemoBound` takes about 45 minutes on 10 cores): `GothamMateIn.lean` (`gotham_not_winsIn_ten`, `gotham_mateIn`, `gotham_specMateIn`, with their axioms checked) and `Gotham10/`, the certificate generated by `scripts/gen_bound.py` (24,994 node theorems). |
| `ChessPerft.lean` | The program `chess-perft`: reads a FEN and a depth, prints the divide and perft. |
| `scripts/` | Untrusted tooling: the certificate generator `gen_cert.py`, the perft differential test, and `check_docstrings.py` (every definition's docstring appears in its spec); `scripts/README.md`. |
| `viewer/` | `gotham.html`, a page that steps through the Gotham proof on a board, built by `scripts/build_viewer.py` from `template.html` and the certificate file. Untrusted, like `scripts/`. |
| `docs/spec/<Module>.md` | English specification of each module, definition by definition. |
| `docs/process/<Module>.md` | Log of the English↔Lean rounds: what changed and why. |
| `docs/reviews/<Module>.md` | File-level design reviews: verdicts, concerns, measurements. |
| `docs/research/*.md` | Background: prior formal work, game theory and Stockfish's role, board representations, Lean engineering, the theorem landscape; `minimality.md`, the measured size of a proof that Black cannot mate the Gotham puzzle within 10. |
| `docs/THEOREMS.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md` | Target theorems, architecture, plan. |
| `docs/VERIFICATION.md` | The verification report of 2026-11-02: builds, counts, trust, differential tests, audit findings and their disposition. |

The throwaway prototype that preceded this library is in `prototype/`. It has a nibble-packed
board and a kernel-checked mate in 11. Its certificate generator has been ported
(`scripts/gen_cert.py` here); it is kept for its measurements, not for its design.
